# Code of Conduct

Canvas MCP welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, as well as representation of the project elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate. Ask for context and explain technical disagreements with evidence.
- Welcome newcomers, accept constructive feedback, and respect people's time and boundaries.
- Protect privacy. Share only data you are entitled to disclose, and remove personal information from examples and reports.
- Focus discussion on improving the project. English fluency is not a requirement for participation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized behavior, personal attacks, doxxing, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Neither are publishing credentials or private academic data, or encouraging others to bypass access controls.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the maintainer privately through the contact options on [Arthur's GitHub profile](https://github.com/arthurcuri), without posting sensitive details in an issue. For security vulnerabilities, use the private reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). If no suitable private contact is available, open an issue asking for a private contact channel without identifying affected people or sharing incident details.

The maintainer will review reports, respect reporter privacy as far as practical, and consider severity, repetition, and context. Responses may include removing content, requesting a correction, issuing a warning, temporarily restricting participation, or banning an account from project spaces. Reports involving the maintainer can also be raised through [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-community-guidelines#reporting-a-violation).
